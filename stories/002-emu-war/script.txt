In 1932, Australia went to war... against birds. After World War One, soldiers were given farmland in Western Australia. Then twenty thousand emus showed up, and started eating everything. So the farmers asked the army for help. The army sent three soldiers, two machine guns, and ten thousand bullets. Easy, right? The emus split into tiny groups, and ran in every direction. So the soldiers put a gun on a truck to chase them. But the truck was too slow, and it shook so much, nobody could aim. After a week, they had barely made a dent. Their commander even said, if we had a military division with the bullet carrying capacity of these birds, it would face any army in the world. So the soldiers packed up, and went home. And that's how Australia lost a war... to birds.
