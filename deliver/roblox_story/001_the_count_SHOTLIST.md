# Roblox Story #1 — "The Count" (horror / mystery, ~70 s)

## Script (I narrate this, you don't need to record any voice)
1. Every night at ten, Mom counts us. One. Two. Three.
2. Me, Leo, and Mia.
3. If the number's ever wrong, nobody moves until she finds the extra one.
4. Last night, she counted four.
5. Leo was in his bed. Mia was in hers. I was in mine.
6. Mom's voice shook. "Nobody move."
7. Then something under my bed whispered my name.
8. I looked down. It was a kid. My hair. My pajamas. My face.
9. It put a finger to its lips… and pointed at me.
10. Mom said, "The real ones know our song."
11. She hummed the first notes. Leo hummed back. Mia hummed back.
12. The kid under my bed hummed it perfectly.
13. Everyone looked at me.
14. I opened my mouth… and nothing came out. I didn't know the song.
15. I had never known the song.
16. And that's when I remembered where I was three weeks ago.
17. Under the bed.
18. Waiting for Mom's real son to fall asleep.
